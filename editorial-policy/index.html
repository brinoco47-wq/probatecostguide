Editorial Policy

Last updated October 2026

Sourcing

Cost and timeline figures must trace to a primary source — a state statute, court fee schedule, or published survey — following our methodology. Secondary sources are never cited as primary evidence.

Dating

Every substantive page carries a "figures checked" date. Undated numbers are treated as errors and fixed.

Independence

Editorial content is produced independently of advertisers. No advertiser can pay for coverage, placement, or a favorable figure.

Corrections

Verified factual errors are corrected promptly, with the correction date noted on the page. Minor typographical fixes are not logged.

AI use

We may use software tools in research and drafting. Every published page is reviewed by a human editor against primary sources before publication. No page publishes on AI output alone.
