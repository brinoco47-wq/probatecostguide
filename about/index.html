About Probate Cost Guide

Last updated October 2026

Probate Cost Guide is an independent research publication answering two questions families ask after a death: how much will probate cost, and how long will it take?

We are researchers and writers — not a law firm, and not attorneys. Nothing on this site is legal advice (see our disclosure).

What we do differently

  • Statutes first. Every cost and timeline figure starts from the controlling state statute or court fee schedule — not from another blog's unsourced claim.
  • Dated figures. Every number carries the month it was checked. Probate fees change; undated numbers are how misinformation spreads.
  • Ranges, not false precision. We publish planning ranges and say plainly what moves a family to the high or low end.
  • Corrections welcome. If you spot an error, tell us — we fix verified errors promptly and note the correction.

How the site is funded

Probate Cost Guide is supported by advertising. We do not sell legal services and do not take referral fees from attorneys. See the full disclosure.
